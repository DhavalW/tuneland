1 · Name your world

This is what fans see on the way in.

2 · Add your songs

Upload an audio file (mp3, m4a, wav · up to 25 MB). It is stored on your own Cloudflare and played inside your world. While it uploads, your browser also listens to it once so the world can breathe with the song.

3 · Tune your areas

Your world is a 3 by 3 grid. The middle is your home stage. Corners are arenas where the glooms live, and where links to other worlds happen. Give each area a color and a song, then save.

4 · Bury loot

Tap the map to pick a spot (the map is your whole world, top-down; darker is lower ground, blue is water). Fans dig it up and get whatever you put inside: a link, a code, a note. Limited drops disappear after that many finds.

5 · Join the Tuneland map

Registering puts your world in the public directory so fans can find it and other artists can link to you. Do this after naming your world.

6 · Link your corners

Pick one of your corners and another registered world. If they say yes, that corner becomes a shared arena: fans walk straight from your land into theirs, and you can both bury loot there.
